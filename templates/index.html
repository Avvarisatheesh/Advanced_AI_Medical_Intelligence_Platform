🩺 AI Medical Intelligence Platform

An AI-powered healthcare application for Medicine Recommendation and Chest X-ray Disease Detection.

👋 Welcome

Welcome to the AI Medical Intelligence Platform. This application combines Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Deep Learning to provide medicine recommendations and chest X-ray disease detection with Explainable AI (Grad-CAM).

📊 Project Statistics

🤖 AI Model: Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)

🎯 Model Accuracy: 89.74%

🩻 Diseases Detected: Normal, Pneumonia

💊 Medicine Recommendation: Enabled

🔥 Explainable AI: Grad-CAM Visualization

🗄️ Database: SQLite

🌐 Framework: Flask

💊 Medicine Recommendation

Enter a medicine name to receive similar medicine recommendations along with therapeutic details, chemical class and possible side effects.

🩻 Chest X-ray Disease Detection

Upload a Chest X-ray image to predict whether it is Normal or Pneumonia.


📌 Supported Formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG
